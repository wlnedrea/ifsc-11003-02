OUR STORY

Made with care, shared with community.

Boulevard Bread Company opened in 2000 and has grown into a local bakery, restaurant, and coffee house serving communities across Central Arkansas. Its approach centers on handcrafted food, quality ingredients, and welcoming places where people can connect.

Good bread, good food, and good company make everyday moments special.
